# ADvanza - Pitch Speaker Notes

Matched to the current 14-slide deck. Approximately 11 minutes, excluding questions. Prepared 7 October 2026.

Use the script as a rehearsal guide, not a word-for-word reading task. Pilot plans and commercial terms remain proposed unless confirmed.

## Slide 01 - Retail Screens. Playable Ads. Connected Offers.

Suggested pace: 45 seconds

### Say

Hello everyone. ADvanza turns retail screens into playable brand experiences. Imagine walking through a shopping mall and finding a short game built around a brand. You play, and when an eligible offer is available, you can save it to your phone and redeem it at a participating store.
We connect three things: the kiosk experience, the shopper wallet, and store redemption. Our planned December 2026 soft launch in Kochi is a pilot to test that complete journey in a real retail environment.

### Delivery cue

Look at the audience first. Point to the kiosk only after explaining the idea. Emphasize: play, save, redeem.

### Transition

To see this complete journey in action, let's watch a short overview film.

## Slide 02 - What is ADvanza (The Ecosystem in Motion)

Suggested pace: 60 seconds

### Say

This video brings the ADvanza ecosystem to life. You see how high-traffic retail screens transition from passive lightboxes into interactive media.
A shopper taps to play a quick branded game, scans the dynamic QR code directly into their mobile wallet, and walks into the store where the discount or offer is verified at checkout.
It's a closed-loop journey: play, claim, visit, and redeem.

### Delivery cue

Start the video. Direct audience attention to the screen during the transition from the kiosk QR scan to the mobile wallet and in-store checkout.

### Transition

Now that you've seen the experience in action, let's examine why current retail advertising falls short.

## Slide 03 - The Problem

Suggested pace: 40 seconds

### Say

Retail environments already contain plenty of advertising. The challenge we are exploring is how to give shoppers a reason to actively engage with a brand, and how to connect that engagement to a clear next action.
A display can communicate a message, but our focus is the journey after someone notices it. Do they interact? Do they save an offer? Is that offer redeemed? We are building ADvanza around those observable steps. We will use the pilot to find out whether this approach is useful to shoppers and partners.

### Delivery cue

Describe the problem as a hypothesis. Do not repeat unsupported claims such as all traditional ads having poor ROI or unmeasured reach.

### Transition

Here is the difference between a fixed advertisement and the experience we are building.

## Slide 04 - Static Advertising vs ADvanza

Suggested pace: 45 seconds

### Say

On the left is a static advertising page: a fixed image that people can see and read. The page itself does not respond to touch or contain gameplay.
On the right is ADvanza. A shopper can interact with a branded game, continue to an available offer on their phone, save it to their wallet, and present it for store redemption.
Our distinction is that connected journey. Static ads can include QR codes, and interactive advertising already exists. We are not claiming that all existing advertising lacks interaction, or that our model has already proven better commercial results.

### Delivery cue

Gesture left, then right. Pause after the words connected journey. The generated images illustrate the concept; they are not installed-site photographs.

### Transition

To make that journey work, we connect three parts.

## Slide 05 - One Experience. Three Connected Parts.

Suggested pace: 40 seconds

### Say

The first part is the retail kiosk, where the shopper plays. The second is the shopper wallet, where an eligible coupon is saved to their account. The third is store redemption, where participating staff verify the coupon.
For shoppers, the proposition is an engaging activity and access to available offers. For brands, it is a playable campaign with engagement and redemption reporting. For venues, it is an interactive retail experience under agreed placement and commercial terms. These parts need to work together, not simply look good in isolation.

### Delivery cue

Follow the chain from kiosk to wallet to store. Give each audience one clear benefit.

### Transition

Now let me explain how a campaign moves through those parts.

## Slide 06 - How It Works

Suggested pace: 40 seconds

### Say

A campaign begins with the brand: its creative, goals, offer and redemption conditions. ADvanza uses those inputs to prepare a short playable experience for the kiosk.
The shopper plays the game. If an eligible coupon is available, a pickup QR lets them continue on their own phone, log in and save it. They can then present that saved coupon at a participating store for staff verification.
The campaign creates measurable steps along the way. A game played is one event; a coupon saved and a coupon redeemed are separate events.

### Delivery cue

Walk through the four stages without reading every bullet. Make eligibility clear; a game win does not guarantee an offer.

### Transition

The physical starting point for all of this is the kiosk.

## Slide 08 - The Shopper Journey

Suggested pace: 60 seconds

### Say

Imagine one shopper noticing the kiosk and choosing to play. After the game, the system checks whether an eligible offer is available.
If it is, the shopper scans the pickup QR with their own phone. They log in or create an account, then save the coupon to their wallet. Later, they present it at a participating store, where staff verify redemption.
ADvanza records the relevant game, save and redemption activity. If no eligible coupon is available, the shopper receives the better-luck-next-time message instead. This slide illustrates the intended flow; the pilot will verify it on real devices, including expiry and connection-recovery cases.

### Delivery cue

Point to each of the six steps. Pause at login and redemption so the audience understands that these are part of the journey.

### Transition

How will we decide whether that journey is working well?

## Slide 09 - Pilot Measurement

Suggested pace: 50 seconds

### Say

We will measure four areas. First, Play: how many started games are completed, alongside session duration. Second, Save: how many pickup sessions result in a coupon saved to the wallet. Third, Redeem: how many saved coupons are redeemed within an agreed window.
Fourth, Operate: panel availability, pickup errors and feedback from venue and store staff. We will agree success thresholds before launch.
These are validation measures, not historical results. A redeemed coupon tells us the offer was used. It does not, on its own, prove an extra store visit, purchase or increase in sales.

### Delivery cue

Use the words play, save, redeem, operate. Do not introduce percentage targets that have not been agreed.

### Transition

Alongside product performance, the commercial responsibilities need to be clear.

## Slide 10 - Business Model

Suggested pace: 45 seconds

### Say

Our proposed commercial model has clear roles. Brands pay for agreed campaign delivery and creative services, define the offer, and receive engagement and redemption reporting.
Venues provide an agreed placement and site access. Revenue share, power, connectivity, installation and maintenance responsibilities are negotiated and documented.
Participating brands or merchants fund and fulfil the offers. ADvanza operates campaign delivery and coupon tracking. We will confirm pilot pricing and cost allocations before launch. At this stage, I am presenting the proposed structure, not claiming signed contracts, established pricing or proven profitability.

### Delivery cue

Be direct about who pays and who fulfils the offer. Do not improvise prices, revenue share percentages or margins.

### Transition

For a venue partner, the practical question is what hosting this experience involves.

## Slide 11 - For Mall Owners

The pilot should help both sides understand shopper interest, staff workload and operating reliability before committing to a larger rollout.

## Slide 12 - Revenue Share: Earn Through Your Existing Screens

Suggested pace: 45 seconds

### Say

If you own or operate touchscreen panels in a hospital, shopping mall, showroom, retail store, or hotel, ADvanza offers a partnership-based revenue-sharing opportunity.
You provide the compatible touchscreen screen infrastructure and placement. ADvanza provides the technology platform, campaign management, and connections to advertisers wanting to engage your visitors.
Brands run interactive campaigns, playable mini-games, and digital reward experiences. When campaigns generate revenue across your screens, you receive an agreed revenue share without having to recruit or manage advertisers independently.

### Delivery cue

Emphasize mutual value: venue brings the screens and footfall; ADvanza brings the technology, interactive campaigns, and brand advertisers.

### Transition

Next, let us look at the commercial mechanics: how a venue progresses from hardware investment to ongoing revenue generation.

## Slide 13 - Venue Model: From Screen Investment to Revenue Generation

Suggested pace: 45 seconds

### Say

Our venue partnership model is structured around a clear commercial journey: Invest Once, Earn Beyond.
When a venue provides or invests in compatible touchscreen infrastructure, that screen cost is treated as recoverable capital.
In Phase 01: Investment Recovery, the venue's agreed share of advertising revenue directly offsets the initial hardware cost until 100% is recovered.
Once the investment is recouped, the venue moves into Phase 02: Continued Earnings. Every subsequent revenue share payout becomes additional earnings, less local operating expenses like electricity and internet.
As an illustration, with a 40% venue share, a ₹50,000 screen recovers its cost once ₹1,25,000 in campaign revenue is collected. Thereafter, the screen generates ongoing returns without the venue needing to manage advertisers.

### Delivery cue

Emphasize the two phases clearly. Use the term "additional earnings after recovery" rather than promising guaranteed profit, acknowledging venue power and internet expenses.

### Transition

I will finish with what ADvanza is and the next step we are asking for.
