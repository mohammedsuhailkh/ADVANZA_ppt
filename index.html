Venue Partnership Model

Invest Once. Earn Beyond.
From Screen Investment to Revenue Generation

ADVANZA’s revenue-sharing model is designed to help venue partners recover their touchscreen investment through advertising revenue and generate additional earnings thereafter.

PHASE 01
Investment Recovery • Advertising revenue share goes directly towards recovering the initial screen cost
100% Recouped →
PHASE 02
Additional Earnings • Subsequent revenue shares become additional ongoing earnings, less operational costs
01 — CAPEX
🖥️

Screen Investment

The venue provides or invests in compatible touchscreen infrastructure. The initial screen cost is treated as the investment to be recovered.

Initial Asset Cost
02 — RECOVERY
📈

Revenue Recovery

The venue receives its agreed share of advertising revenue generated through its screens, which pays down the initial hardware investment.

Ad Revenue Offset
03 — BREAKEVEN
🎯

Investment Recovered

Once the agreed screen investment amount has been fully recovered, the venue crosses breakeven and moves directly into the earnings phase.

100% Payback Complete
04 — YIELD
💎

Continued Earnings

Every subsequent revenue share received by the venue becomes additional earnings, after applicable operating and maintenance expenses.

Ongoing Additional Revenue
💡
Illustrative Example (40% Venue Share): A ₹50,000 screen with a 40% venue share recovers its cost with ₹1,25,000 in attributable campaign revenue. All subsequent payouts become additional earnings.
⚖️
Commercial Clarification: Termed “additional earnings after investment recovery” rather than 100% profit to account for venue electricity, internet and maintenance expenses without promising speculative returns.
