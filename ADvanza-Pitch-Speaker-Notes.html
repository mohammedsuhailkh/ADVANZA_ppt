The Problem

Suggested speaking time: 40 seconds

What to say

Retail environments already contain plenty of advertising. The challenge we are exploring is how to give shoppers a reason to actively engage with a brand, and how to connect that engagement to a clear next action.

A display can communicate a message, but our focus is the journey after someone notices it. Do they interact? Do they save an offer? Is that offer redeemed? We are building ADvanza around those observable steps. We will use the pilot to find out whether this approach is useful to shoppers and partners.

Slide 05 of 13

How It Works

Suggested speaking time: 40 seconds

What to say

A campaign begins with the brand: its creative, goals, offer and redemption conditions. ADvanza uses those inputs to prepare a short playable experience for the kiosk.

The shopper plays the game. If an eligible coupon is available, a pickup QR lets them continue on their own phone, log in and save it. They can then present that saved coupon at a participating store for staff verification.

The campaign creates measurable steps along the way. A game played is one event; a coupon saved and a coupon redeemed are separate events.

Delivery cue

Walk through the four stages without reading every bullet. Make eligibility clear; a game win does not guarantee an offer.

Slide 07 of 13

The Shopper Journey

Suggested speaking time: 60 seconds

What to say

Imagine one shopper noticing the kiosk and choosing to play. After the game, the system checks whether an eligible offer is available.

If it is, the shopper scans the pickup QR with their own phone. They log in or create an account, then save the coupon to their wallet. Later, they present it at a participating store, where staff verify redemption.

ADvanza records the relevant game, save and redemption activity. If no eligible coupon is available, the shopper receives the better-luck-next-time message instead. This slide illustrates the intended flow; the pilot will verify it on real devices, including expiry and connection-recovery cases.

Delivery cue

Point to each of the six steps. Pause at login and redemption so the audience understands that these are part of the journey.

Slide 08 of 13

Pilot Measurement

Suggested speaking time: 50 seconds

What to say

We will measure four areas. First, Play: how many started games are completed, alongside session duration. Second, Save: how many pickup sessions result in a coupon saved to the wallet. Third, Redeem: how many saved coupons are redeemed within an agreed window.

Fourth, Operate: panel availability, pickup errors and feedback from venue and store staff. We will agree success thresholds before launch.

These are validation measures, not historical results. A redeemed coupon tells us the offer was used. It does not, on its own, prove an extra store visit, purchase or increase in sales.

Delivery cue

Use the words play, save, redeem, operate. Do not introduce percentage targets that have not been agreed.

Transition

Alongside product performance, the commercial responsibilities need to be clear.

If you own or operate touchscreen panels in a hospital, shopping mall, showroom, retail store or hotel, ADvanza offers a partnership-based revenue-sharing opportunity.

You provide the compatible touchscreen screen infrastructure and placement. ADvanza provides the technology platform, campaign management, and connections to advertisers wanting to engage your visitors.

Slide 12 of 13

Venue Model: From Screen Investment to Revenue Generation

Suggested speaking time: 45 seconds

What to say

Our venue partnership model is structured around a clear commercial journey: Invest Once, Earn Beyond.

When a venue provides or invests in compatible touchscreen infrastructure, that screen cost is treated as recoverable capital.

In Phase 01: Investment Recovery, the venue's agreed share of advertising revenue directly offsets the initial hardware cost until 100% is recovered. Once the investment is recouped, the venue moves into Phase 02: Continued Earnings. Every subsequent revenue share payout becomes additional earnings, less local operating expenses like electricity and internet.

As an illustration, with a 40% venue share, a ₹50,000 screen recovers its cost once ₹1,25,000 in campaign revenue is collected. Thereafter, the screen generates ongoing returns without the venue needing to manage advertisers.

Delivery cue

Emphasize the two phases clearly. Use the term "additional earnings after recovery" rather than promising guaranteed profit, acknowledging venue power and internet expenses.

Transition

I will finish with what ADvanza is and the next step we are asking for.
